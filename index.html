How this works

Architects use the same design vocabulary as artists. Your job is to find that vocabulary in real buildings, and to show exactly where you see it by placing markers on the photos.

  1. Part 1: For each of 6 buildings, click the photo to drop 3 numbered markers. For each marker, choose the element or principle it shows and write one sentence about what at that spot shows it.
  2. Part 2: Compare two buildings and explain how balance changes the way they feel.
  3. Part 3: Find the focal point of one building and explain what pulls your eye there.
  4. Finish: Click Save as PDF at the bottom and upload the PDF to Canvas.
Your work saves automatically in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back on the same laptop. Typing must be your own: pasting into the answer boxes is turned off.
